# Short finding title

- Status: proposed | applied | project-only | deferred | rejected | reverted (choose one)
- Origin: P-n/task or a named user correction; date
- Scope: shared kit or named project; affected role/stage
- Evidence: exact file/artifact/check or conversation reference; avoid copying private transcripts
- Expected / observed: concise observable difference
- Impact: rework, failure, missing capability or measured cost; unknown where not measured
- Baseline: kit Git revision and affected project/artifact revision if relevant
- Hypothesis / lesson: distinguish explanation from demonstrated facts
- Change: smallest patch or project setting; files and why the scope is appropriate
- Checks: commands/cases actually run, outcomes and unverified behavior
- Adoption: when to load the change; impact on in-flight work and existing plans
- Undo: change/commit to reverse; preserve unrelated edits
- Next: owner/action or none; reason if deferred/rejected
