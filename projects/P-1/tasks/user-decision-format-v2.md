# User decision: format v2 after the competitor study (2026-10-18)

Context: research/competitor-shorts-v1.md compared Hẻm Threads (the user's two examples) with our channel. The user had called the latest batch "tạm được thôi chứ chưa xuất sắc lắm".

The user's answers in chat:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Voice | **"A: Đọc theo card (~60–70%)"**: read the card text verbatim (teen-code expanded), with at most one 2–6-word aside per card, placed after the quote. Never explain the joke. |
| Hook strip, spoken CTA, logo outro | **"Bỏ hết, kết ở câu chốt"**: no handwritten hook strip and no spoken CTA (the question goes in the description). Replace the logo outro with a small moving text watermark. End on the punchline so the video loops. |
| Memes | **"1–3 meme to"**: 1–3 large reaction beats (60–90% of frame width) in a 1.5–2.5s pause after a punchline, with a laugh or rimshot SFX. An optional 1–2-word label. No micro or text stickers. |
| Background | **"Đồng ý, tải clip Pexels"**: download about 6 bright Pexels clips (cooking, street food, crafts; Pexels License), cut every 4–8s. Show one large card in the centre. |

Consequences:
- **Rights protocol rule 5 changes** for P-1 from this date. Verbatim may reach about 60–75% of voice time (was 35% or less), and original speech may drop to about 25% or more (was 50% or more). The user knowingly accepts the higher YouTube Partner Program "reused content" risk; see the earlier risk stance in tasks/user-risk-stance-v1.md.
- **Rules that stay:** avatars blurred; private names, minors, politics and sexual content rejected; no film/TV or anime art; licence-clean assets only; credits in the description; profanity blurred on cards and not read aloud.
- **Brand changes:** the brand outro and handle strip are replaced by a watermark (brand.json). This partly reverses tasks/user-decision-brand-v1.md, by the user's choice.
- **Scope:** engine changes in P-1 engine-v1 and new or re-cut episodes. Publishing remains the user's action unless they approve a specific upload.
