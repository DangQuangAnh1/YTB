# Channel name and SEO: BA research, 2026-10-17

Read-only research. Autocomplete data came from suggestqueries (hl=vi, gl=vn) and handle checks from public YouTube/TikTok pages. **V** means verified on 2026-10-17; **I** means inferred.

## What people search for

- **"đọc comment / đọc cmt"** is the closest match to the format. YouTube suggests "đọc comment threads", "đọc cmt tiktok", "nghiện/thích đọc comment" and "tối đọc thread"; Google suggests "đọc cmt trên threads" (V).
- **"cmt hài / comment hài / bình luận hài"** shows real demand for comment compilations (V).
- **"threads"** is the strongest niche anchor: "threads hài", "kể chuyện threads" (V).
- **"hóng"** is pulled toward showbiz and drama gossip, which is press-like (V).
- **The brand names themselves** ("hóng cmt", "lướt giùm", "rep gì đây") return no suggestions. "hóng rep" pulls up gardening videos about "hồng rệp" (V).

## Ranking factors

- **YouTube search** ranks on relevance (title, tags, description, content), engagement and quality. Channel name and handle are not listed as factors (V).
- **TikTok For You feed** depends on interactions, captions, sounds and hashtags (V).

So the name only needs to be memorable and searchable on its own. The SEO work belongs in titles, captions, on-screen text, voiceover and playlists (I).

## Naming constraints

- **Meta trademark:** keep it out of the username. Descriptive mentions such as "chuyện trên Threads" in descriptions and titles are fine. Add "Không liên kết với Meta" (V for Instagram; I for Threads).
- **Decree 147/2024:** account names may not contain báo, đài, tạp chí, tin, tin tức, truyền hình, truyền thông, thông tấn, or their foreign equivalents (V via law journal).
