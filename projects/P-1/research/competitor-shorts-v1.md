# Competitor Shorts study v1: Hẻm Threads vs Đọc Cmt Giùm (BA agent, 2026-10-18)

User request (chat, 2026-10-18): the latest videos are "tạm được thôi chứ chưa xuất sắc lắm"; study how other channels do it, starting from youtube.com/shorts/YczsCcXHD8g and youtube.com/shorts/Ryd5bQNogPU. Both examples turned out to come from the same channel, **Hẻm Threads (@HẻmThreads)**.

- **Scope.** Research only. Read-only browsing in the built-in browser pane, signed out. Nothing was liked, commented, subscribed, downloaded or posted. No project file other than this one was changed, and scripts/agency.py was not run.
- **Marks.** **(V)** = observed on the page, in a sampled frame, in YouTube's auto-captions or in a local file. **(I)** = inference or opinion.
- **Method.** Metadata from the watch page's player response and page data. Transcripts from YouTube's Vietnamese auto-captions (ASR, so wording can be slightly off). Visuals: paused the `<video>`, set `currentTime`, and screenshotted 15–20 timestamps per video at 1080×1920 source quality. Comments: YouTube's default "Top comments" order.
- **Not observed: audio.** Playback was muted and nothing was listened to. Voice identity, music genre, SFX and mix levels are **unverified**. Where this report mentions music or laughter it relies on the ASR tags "[âm nhạc]" and "[tiếng cười]" (V as tags, I as sound).
- **Privacy.** Handles of private Threads users, children's names and the competitor's contact phone numbers are deliberately left out of this report.

## 4. Comparison by dimension

1. **Hook.**
   - Theirs: the source's own first sentence, read at 0.0s over the card, which is visible at frame 0 (V). For type (a) the first sentence carries the conflict and a number ("800 triệu") (V).
   - Ours: a narrator line plus a title strip, with the source arriving at 4–14s in the local renders (V).
   - (I) Our hook paraphrases a hook that the source already has.
2. **Story selection.**
   - Theirs: (a) dilemmas that end in a question; (b) one absurd chat with a twist; (c) prompt-post compilations. Replies are standalone one-liners with 1K–23K likes (V).
   - Ours: similar warm or funny family chats, pets and shops (V).
   - (I) Selection is close. The difference is that they let the source carry the joke.
3. **Pacing.**
   - Theirs: either continuous reading at about 4.3 syll/s with background cuts every 4–8s (Hẻm 1), or a visual event every 1.5–3s with 2–4s silent meme beats (Hẻm 2) (V).
   - Ours: about 3.8 syll/s with no beats longer than 0.85s. Card holds run up to 17.5s while the narrator talks (V).
   - (I) Ours feels like steady talk; theirs has a reveal-then-laugh rhythm.
4. **Visual density and readability.**
   - Theirs: one card (sometimes 2 replies) at about 88% width, on a bright, busy but non-text background, plus one tiny watermark (V).
   - Ours: card, collapsed strips, title strip, @handle, CTA line and stickers on a dimmed background (V).
   - (I) Ours is more "designed" but busier, and the dim background makes the frame look heavy in a feed of bright videos.
5. **Text vs voice.**
   - Theirs: voice = card text, with teen-code normalized (V).
   - Ours: voice ≠ card text for 71–85% of the runtime (V).
   - (I) This is the most likely meaning of "đừng có nhét thêm chữ vào".
6. **Memes.**
   - Theirs: 0–3 per video, very large, in voice pauses, sometimes with a one-word label ("cô giáo"), with a laugh track (V). All are third-party images (V).
   - Ours: 6–8 small emoji or text stickers per video, each with a pop SFX (V).
   - (I) Theirs land as punchlines; ours read as decoration.
7. **Music and SFX.**
   - Theirs: a continuous music bed (ASR tags) and a laugh at the meme (V tags; sound unverified).
   - Ours: a music bed plus a whoosh on every card and a pop on every reaction, 10–15 SFX per video (V props).
   - (I) Likely busier than theirs. This can't be confirmed without listening.
8. **Length.**
   - Theirs: 14–122s, median about 45–51s (V).
   - Ours: 37–58s (V).
   - (I) Not the main gap. Our dead tail (4.6s after the last word) is, because Shorts loop.
9. **Title and thumbnail.**
   - Theirs: emoji or one word plus hashtags, empty description (V). They rely entirely on the Shorts feed (I).
   - Ours: descriptive hook titles following youtube-seo-v1 (V).
   - Shorts feed thumbnails are mostly not shown; the first frame acts as the thumbnail (I).
   - (I) Keep our titles. They don't hurt, and they help search and the channel page.
10. **CTA.**
    - Theirs: none. The source's own question ("Mọi người nghĩ em nên…?") drives the comments (V).
    - Ours: every video ends "Kể nghe đi, câu hay nhất lên tập sau" (V), which the review already called repetitive.
11. **Branding.**
    - Theirs: a moving semi-transparent text watermark and the own hashtag; no intro or outro (V).
    - Ours: a persistent handle and hook strip plus a 2.4s logo outro (V).
12. **Cadence.**
    - Theirs: 2 a day at fixed lunch and evening slots for 3 months (V).
    - Ours: 1 public video (V).
    - (I) Cadence and channel history probably explain more of the 20× view gap than any single creative change.

## 5. Recommendations

### 5.1 Top 5 changes (engine and scripts), in priority order

Expected impacts are inferences (I) and must be validated with retention graphs on at least 3 uploads per variant.

1. **Open on the source at 0.0s.**
   - What to change:
     - The first card is fully visible on frame 0.
     - The first line is the post's own sentence, read verbatim from about 0.1s (`LEAD` 0.5 → 0.1 in build-timeline.mjs).
     - Remove the cold-open narrator line.
     - Delete the hook strip, or show it only in the first frame as a thumbnail-style title for under 1s (`Hook` in index.tsx; per-episode `"hook_strip": false`).
     - Pick posts whose first sentence already has the conflict or number.
   - Expected impact: higher 3-second hold and swipe-away rate (I). This is the cheapest change.
2. **Make the voice follow the screen. Never explain the joke.**
   - Scripts: each card gets its own text read (teen-code expanded).
   - Commentary drops from 100–150 words to **at most one aside per card, 2–6 words, placed after the quote and never before it**. Examples: "Ủa?", "Toang thật.", "Mẹ đỉnh."
   - Delete announce-lines ("Cư dân mạng…", "Tác giả tiết lộ…") and explain-lines ("…đúng là thiên tài ngôn ngữ").
   - Expected impact: removes the text/voice mismatch the only viewer complained about (I).
   - **Conflict:** this breaks rights-protocol-v1 rule 5 (verbatim ≤35%, original ≥50%), which exists for YouTube's reused-content (monetization) policy. Hẻm Threads' 100%-verbatim style is exactly what that policy excludes, and their monetization status is unknown.
   - Two options for the user (decision needed, §5.5):
     - **A.** "Read-first": verbatim about 60–70% with short asides, accepting YPP risk.
     - **B.** Stay at ≥50% original, but move the originality into structure: our own 1-line framing of a **multi-source compilation**, one aside per card and our own mascot beats.
3. **End on the punchline and loop.**
   - Remove the spoken CTA, `TAIL` 2.2s and the 2.4s logo outro (per-episode `"outro": false` already exists; also set brand.json `outro_s`).
   - The last frame is the funniest reply plus one big reaction, then a hard cut.
   - Move the CTA question into the description or a pinned comment, written by the user when uploading. Prefer posts whose source already ends in a question.
   - Expected impact: no 4.6s dead tail (8–12% of each video), so a higher share viewed and more replays (I).
4. **Fewer, bigger meme beats, in pauses.**
   - New preset tier "beat": **1–3 per video**, Fluent animated emoji or our own mascot (both licence-clean) at **600–900px wide** (55–85% of the frame), for 1.5–2.5s.
   - Each lands on a 1.5–2.5s **voice pause** after the punchline (`pause_before` on the next line), with one laugh or rimshot SFX (CC0).
   - Allow a 1–2-word OFL label on the beat (the "cô giáo" device), at most 2 per video.
   - Drop micro text stickers and most micro emoji (hài preset `max` 8 → 3).
   - Cut whoosh-per-card. Keep a whoosh only on the twist card.
   - Expected impact: punchlines read as jokes instead of decoration (I). This is a preset change and needs user approval (meme-rights-and-repos-v1 §4.6 #1).
5. **One card, bigger, on a brighter moving background.**
   - Show only the active card (`STRIPS` → 0) at about 92% width, with 2 short replies allowed together.
   - Background brightness 0.5 → about 0.9, with a drop shadow on the card for contrast.
   - Replace the single looped clip with a **playlist of 4–6 Pexels food or "satisfying process" clips cut every 4–8s**: cooking, street food, crafts, all licence-clean.
   - Swap the persistent @handle for a small semi-transparent text watermark that changes corner every 8–10s.
   - Expected impact: a lighter, feed-native look and better text legibility on phones (I).

- **Unblurred avatars, private full names and children's names.** Hẻm 2 shows a teacher's full name and several children's names. These break rights rule 2 (no minors) and the personal-data law (91/2025).

- **Irrelevant trend hashtags** (#fyp, #xuhuongyoutube, #viral). youtube-seo-v1 §4f warns against misleading or irrelevant metadata.
