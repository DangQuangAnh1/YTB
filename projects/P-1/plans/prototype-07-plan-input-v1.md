The user's 7/10 feedback is in tasks/user-feedback-p06v2-v1.md. Everything here is private drafts with zero added spend: no publishing, no channel creation, no automation, and no voice cloning.

1. **BA, three parallel read-only research agents:**
   - (a) high-star AI video/meme repos and plan/idea repos useful for P-1;
   - (b) Vietnamese TTS voices per mood, with licenses;

2. **Production:** audition the local VieNeu preset voices (plus free alternatives where the license allows) on funny and sad lines, then map a voice to each mood preset.
3. **Production:** redesign the composition based on 1(c):
   - real-footage or texture background per mood, from licensed free stock;
   - a Vietnamese-capable open font (OFL);
   - creator-native caption styling;
   - channel identity elements.
   Re-render the existing funny video as prototype-06 v3.
4. **Content and Production:** make one new demo (prototype-07) from a fresh public Threads post, preferably with a different mood to test mood switching. Keep real cards and identities, record provenance, and run whisper.cpp captions.
5. **Review:** two independent model reviewers, an audience persona and an SEO/marketing persona. They score the exact outputs and are labelled simulations, not market data.
6. **PM:** consolidate into a final report with results, checks, gaps and recommended next steps.
