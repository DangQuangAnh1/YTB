# Music preferences

- Primary genre: cinematic electronic, future bass, synthwave or cyberpunk EDM.
- Vocal preference: female vocal first; favour airy, ethereal, cinematic or
  emotional vocal phrases that support the reveal rather than dominate it.
- Video fit: choose a compelling 15-second excerpt, then cut and align it to
  the transformation; a drop does not need to occur at exactly 00:07.
- Rights: retain the Pixabay track URL, filename and licence/source note.
- Avoid a Content ID-registered track when a comparable alternative is
  available.
