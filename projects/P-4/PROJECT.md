# P-4 — Morphix 3D VN

Daily 15-second 3D-transformation video workflow: the creator produces the
video from a supplied prompt; P-4 keeps the daily prompt brief and prepares
the final video with a creator-supplied, licensed 15-second music file.

- Plan of record: `plans/` (registered from the workspace `planp4`).
- Daily prompts: `daily-prompts/`.
- Private source videos: `media/source-video/`.
- Private licensed music: `media/music-15s/`.
- Private final exports: `media/output/`.

Read `WORKFLOW.md` before adding media. Operational records are `STATE.json`
and generated `HANDOFF.md`.
