# Storyboard and packaging

Titles/thumbnails must promise what the content actually delivers. SEO supports discovery; it does not justify false claims or keyword stuffing. Keep source/use evidence for borrowed material.

Storyboard meaningful sections with scene ID, approximate time, narration, on-screen text and visual mechanism. Narration estimates differ from measured voice timing. Choose Shorts/ordinary-video pacing for the current brief, not another project's habits.

For adaptation, retain the viewer need but create a distinct explanation/story. Translation/crop/subtitles do not by themselves establish permission or monetization suitability.

After review, fix named findings and affected dependencies into a new file. Explain changed scene IDs; do not silently reuse stale audio/visuals after changing a script. The bundled renderer accepts the shape in contracts/render-script.schema.json and only draws a queue diagram; do not force unrelated stories into it.
