# Rubrics and evidence

Research: important claims supported by accessible sources, observation/inference distinguished, freshness, counterexamples, feasible recommendation. Unretrieved pages remain partial.

Script checks: brief_alignment, source_accuracy, story_coherence. Inspect the audience promise, factual claims, progression/payoff and source support.

Video checks: brief_alignment, visual_review, audio_review, media_decode. Inspect meaningful scenes/transitions, legibility, timing and actual audio. A decoder log proves technical decoding only. Sampled frames do not cover every scene. Missing checks stay unverified.

Evidence may be a decoder log, saved source note, frame/scene review or listening report with actual target hashes/time ranges. Declare reviewer and self_review/independent_review/human_review honestly. Helper validation is not a fact oracle or human authentication.

Use passed only after performing the check, failed with evidence of the issue, and unverified with what is needed. Never claim viewing/listening if the host exposed only metadata. A QA pass covers listed artifacts, not permission to publish or commercial success.
