# Market scouting

Take audience/market/format if known, accessible sources, constraints and the decision to support. If the niche is open, propose directions rather than demanding the user supply the idea.

Pilot setting: screen up to about 10 candidates, deepen up to 3, present 1–3 supported opportunities. Adjust for the task; do not invent filler. Use templates/OPPORTUNITY.json and SOURCES.json with unknown fields null.

Retain Chinese/original titles and keywords with Vietnamese interpretation. Login/region/blank pages are access limitations, not permission to bypass or import cookies. Search snippets are partial evidence; downloader support is not a trend-discovery system.

Compare viewer need, observable recent signal, target-market fit, original angle, effort/capability and uncertainty. No invented viral probabilities or ROI scores. Velocity requires two compatible timestamped observations. Compare like formats, inspect counterexamples and avoid treating one outlier as a niche.

Original content translates the need into a distinct explanation/story. Reuse/adaptation needs source/use evidence and current platform conditions; permission and monetization eligibility are different questions. Do not give a legal conclusion about an unseen clip. Deduplicate IDs/ideas, retain rejection reasons and refresh conditions. PM/user choose scope before production.
