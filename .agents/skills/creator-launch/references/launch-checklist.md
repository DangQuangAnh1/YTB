# Launch checklist

These are manual steps. The agent may prepare files and instructions but must not do these without explicit, per-action user approval.

1. The user creates or claims the channel or account under the chosen name and handles (YouTube, TikTok), and accepts the platform terms.
2. Upload the avatar and banner, write the bio (what the channel is, credits policy, takedown contact) and add links.
3. Monetization readiness. Review the YouTube Partner Program "reused content" and "inauthentic content" rules and TikTok originality rules against the format: original commentary of at least 50%, varied structure.
4. First uploads.
   - Manual upload through the official apps is the default.
   - Optional automation only after explicit authorization: the official YouTube Data API (OAuth; unverified apps upload as private; quota limits apply).
   - Avoid unofficial TikTok automation (terms risk).
5. Keep a takedown register and respond within 24h.
6. Track performance only once analytics access exists. v0 has no analytics integration.
