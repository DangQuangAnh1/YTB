# Brand kit

1. **Name shortlist.**
   - Offer 5–8 names: short, easy to say in Vietnamese, and describing what the channel does.
   - Avoid platform trademarks (for example "Threads" or "TikTok") and press-style words such as "báo" or "tin tức" (Decree 147 risk).
   - Check handles read-only through public URLs (`youtube.com/@x`, `tiktok.com/@x`).
   - A 404 or "couldn't find" result only means the handle *appears* free. It may still be reserved.
2. **Logo concepts.**
   - Make 2–3 original vector marks (SVG in Remotion, OFL fonts, own drawings).
   - Show each one as an avatar circle and as a wordmark, on dark and on light backgrounds.
   - Do not trace other brands. AI images go through a commercially licensed model only, with the license recorded.
3. **Exports after the user picks one.**
   - Avatar: 800×800 PNG, readable at about 98px (YouTube) and as a small TikTok circle.
   - Banner: 2560×1440 with the 1546×423 YouTube safe area.
   - Watermark/handle: the in-video identity element.
4. **Outro.**
   - A 2–3s Remotion component (logo pop, handle, series line or CTA), added to engine episodes as an optional tail.
   - Keep it short. Shorts have no end screens, so the outro is part of the video itself.
5. **Record.** Save the brand decision (name, logo id, colours, fonts) in project records together with the user's excerpt.
